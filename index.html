Leadership

We created a black and white prototype of the application using the feedback and criticism that we got from the first feedback loop. In this prototype, we included these important pages: Home page, where users can find reviews and promotions and foster a community; Search page, where users can look up services based on their location; Profile page, where users can update their personal information and see their booking information; Chat page, where users can chat with their artists or their clients.

Certifications

  • Booking flow: Users attempted to book from the homepage instead of the intended booking section
  • Information availability: Users expected to see the average response time and frequent active hours displayed on profiles, but these were not presented
  • Tab bar visibility: Users had difficulty finding the chat function and overlooking the bottom tab bar
  • Chat function: Users expected to have chat boxes directly under posts or booked services, as well as an indication of average reply time, but these were not presented
  • Token system: The “Available Tokens” feature was confusing as users didn’t understand what tokens were, how to use or spend them, or how tokens were subtracted after booking
